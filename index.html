The designer behind the work

Design isn't decoration. It's how a brand talks.

I'm a graphic designer from Dhaka with a journalism background — which means I think about who's reading, not just how it looks. At PsyCoach Bangladesh I turn brand ideas into social media that actually gets engagement.

Every piece starts with one question: what should this make someone feel, or do? Then I build it clean and sharp. When it doesn't hold attention, I redo it. No egos, just results.
